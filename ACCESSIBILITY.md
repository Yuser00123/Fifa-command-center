# ACCESSIBILITY PROTOCOLS (WCAG 2.1 AA)

This platform is engineered to offer fully inclusive operation for organizers, volunteers, and fans, specifically accommodating physical, visual, or hearing impairments.

---

## ♿ 1. Step-Free Pathway Engine

- The system features a dedicated "Step-Free Pathway Mode" inside the wayfinding module.
- Activates specialized routing steps that exclude stairs, congested ramps, or manual doors, focusing exclusively on elevators, priority lifts, and tactile step-free corridors.

---

## 🎨 2. High Contrast & Readable Color Space

- Font sizes and contrast ratios are compliant with WCAG 2.1 AA requirements.
- Uses high-contrast forest green and emerald accent combinations to ensure outstanding visibility, even under heavy glare or sunlight conditions in active stadium arenas.

---

## ⌨️ 3. Semantic Layouts & Screen Readers

- Core components are wrapped in semantic landmarks: `<header>`, `<main>`, `<nav>`, `<section>`, and `<footer>`.
- Interactive selectors contain distinct `aria-label` or `role="checkbox"` markup to guide assistive screen reading engines.
- Focus rings and outline parameters ensure fully keyboard-navigable operations.
